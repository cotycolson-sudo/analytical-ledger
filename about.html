About

Welcome

The Analytical Ledger explores the intersection of artificial intelligence, data ethics, and modern business logic. In an era where data-driven decision-making shapes organizational strategy, understanding the ethical implications and practical applications of AI is essential for business leaders, analysts, and technologists alike.

My goal is to bridge the gap between technical AI concepts and their real-world business applications, while critically examining the ethical frameworks that should guide our use of these powerful tools.
