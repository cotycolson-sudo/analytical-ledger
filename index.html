From Invisible to Essential: The Rise of Lunar New Year in America

A tea shop owner in Kent told me nobody would buy traditional teaware. Two weeks later, I found gaiwans at Target. Using Census data and Google Trends, I built a dashboard to understand why—and discovered a demographic story major retailers read more carefully than small shops. Asian Americans grew 13% from 2020–2024, and Washington state ranks third nationally for Lunar New Year search interest. The demand was never absent. It was just invisible.
